HOW TO USE: canvas-svg-animator
================================

PURPOSE
  Transforms static SVG vector graphics into high-performance animated widgets
  with glowing neon traces, laser outline drawing, continuous spins, and pulse blooms.

CAPABILITIES
  - Laser-Trace Drawing: Vector lines draw themselves sequentially on screen
  - Pulse / Neon Glow: Ambient breathing neon glow and drop-shadow bloom
  - Radar Spin: Continuous smooth mechanical or sweep rotation
  - Combo Mode: Sequential laser draw followed by infinite ambient neon breathing
  - Outputs standalone interactive HTML showcase player + clean copy-paste CSS keyframes

USE CASES
  - Bring flat UI icons or logo vectors to life with a futuristic laser draw effect
  - Animate radar sweeps, compass dials, or tape reels
  - Add ambient neon breathing effects to HUD viewports and synth faceplates

IDEAS
  - Combine radar rotation with laser-trace drawing for high-tech HUD radars
  - Apply ambient neon breathing to audio hardware knobs and VU meters
  - Embed animated SVG sprites into dashboard headers for active telemetry feeds
